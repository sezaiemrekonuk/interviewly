Senior Backend Engineer (Node.js / TypeScript)

About the role
We are looking for a senior backend engineer to own the services behind our
scheduling platform. You will design HTTP APIs, shape the data model, and keep a
Postgres-backed system fast and predictable under load. The team is small, so you
will be close to the product decisions and to the people using what you build.

What you will do
- Design and build TypeScript services on Node.js, deployed as containers.
- Model data in PostgreSQL: migrations, indexing, query performance.
- Own reliability for the services you ship: structured logs, metrics, alerts.
- Review code and mentor two mid-level engineers.
- Work with the frontend team on API contracts before either side writes code.

What we are looking for
- 5+ years building backend services, at least 2 with TypeScript or Node.js.
- Strong SQL and a real working knowledge of relational modelling.
- Experience with asynchronous work: queues, retries, idempotency.
- Comfortable with Docker and CI pipelines.
- Clear written communication — most of our design work happens in documents.

Nice to have
- Experience with Redis, BullMQ, or a comparable job runner.
- Exposure to observability tooling (OpenTelemetry, Elasticsearch, Grafana).
- Having been on call for a system you built.

We work remotely with two overlapping hours in Central European Time.
